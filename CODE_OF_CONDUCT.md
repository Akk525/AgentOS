# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment or discriminatory language of any kind
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing someone's private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a GitHub issue marked `[conduct]` or by contacting the maintainers directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding reporters.
